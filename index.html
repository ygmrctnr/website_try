Physiological Conduction

Intact oligodendroglial myelin increases internodal membrane resistance and lowers effective capacitance, enabling rapid saltatory propagation. Conduction velocity depends strongly on axon diameter, myelin geometry, temperature, and channel distribution; Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase maintains the ion gradients that support resting excitability.

READY · PRESYNAPTIC TERMINALGLUTAMATE · AMPA
01 · TRIGGER

Ca²⁺ couples electricity to secretion

Terminal depolarization opens voltage-gated Ca²⁺ channels. Local Ca²⁺ entry activates the vesicle-fusion machinery and produces rapid exocytosis.

02 · CURRENT TRANSMITTER

Glutamate · fast excitation

Glutamate binding to AMPA receptors rapidly increases cation conductance. At typical resting voltages, inward current depolarizes the postsynaptic membrane and generates an EPSP.

03 · CLEARANCE

EAAT-mediated uptake

Excitatory amino-acid transporters remove glutamate from extracellular space; astrocytes are major participants in uptake and glutamate–glutamine cycling.

Core principle

A transmitter is not intrinsically “excitatory” or “inhibitory” in every context; the effect depends on receptor type, ionic gradients and membrane voltage.

SYSTEMS ARCHITECTURE & BIOPHYSICAL SPECIFICATION

Production Architecture, GLSL Shaders & Primary Literature

1. FRONTEND & STATE STACK

Static HTML + Vanilla JS + Tailwind

UI/UX Layer: Single-file HTML5 application with Tailwind CSS design tokens, Three.js r128, OrbitControls, and Chart.js 3.9.1.
Biophysical State: Vanilla JavaScript stores membrane voltage and Hodgkin–Huxley gating variables (`Vm`, `m`, `h`, `n`) plus an accelerated teaching-state model of the Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase. Rendering is performed with `requestAnimationFrame` and direct Three.js object updates.

2. STRUCTURAL REFERENCES & PROCEDURAL PROTEINS

Topology-Informed Teaching Models

Structural Visualization: PDB entries are cited as scientific references for topology and conformational states, while the rendered proteins are deliberately simplified Three.js teaching models. They are not atom-for-atom reconstructions, molecular-dynamics trajectories, or direct renderings of deposited coordinates.

3. PROCEDURAL WEBGL SHADERS

Dynamic Lesion Transitions

Procedural Visualization: Vertex and fragment shaders use lightweight hash-based value noise, Fresnel shading, and parametric deformations to create illustrative lesion morphs. These are explanatory graphics, not quantitative histopathology simulations or reaction–diffusion models.

CORE GLSL SHADER: PROCEDURAL VALUE NOISE + ILLUSTRATIVE LESION MORPHS 
// Updated 2026 Vertex & Fragment Shader (Active in 3D Viewport)
uniform float uTime;
uniform float uApWavePos;       // Normalized [0..1] saltatory spike position
uniform float uPathologyType;   // 1 = AD PHF-Tau/Aβ, 2 = MS Paranodal Blistering, 3 = Stroke TRPM4 Beading
uniform float uPathologySev;    // Smoothly lerped [0..1] lesion expression state

varying vec3 vNormal;
varying vec3 vViewPosition;
varying vec3 vWorldPos;
varying float vLesionMask;

void main() {
    vec3 N = normalize(vNormal);
    vec3 V = normalize(vViewPosition);
    float fresnel = pow(1.0 - max(dot(N, V), 0.0), 2.4);

    // Healthy Subsurface Scattering vs. Disease-Specific Histopathology Chromophores
    vec3 healthyRGB = vec3(0.28, 0.35, 0.65) + vec3(0.58, 0.68, 0.98) * fresnel * 1.45;
    vec3 adTauRGB   = mix(vec3(0.18, 0.14, 0.22), vec3(0.96, 0.62, 0.11), smoothstep(0.35, 0.82, vLesionMask));
    vec3 msLesionRGB= mix(vec3(0.35, 0.18, 0.28), vec3(0.95, 0.24, 0.37), smoothstep(0.30, 0.78, vLesionMask));
    vec3 strokeRGB  = mix(vec3(0.15, 0.22, 0.32), vec3(0.88, 0.18, 0.32), fresnel * 1.6);

    vec3 targetPathRGB = (uPathologyType < 1.5) ? adTauRGB : ((uPathologyType < 2.5) ? msLesionRGB : strokeRGB);
    vec3 finalRGB = mix(healthyRGB, targetPathRGB, uPathologySev * smoothstep(0.15, 0.85, vLesionMask));
    gl_FragColor = vec4(finalRGB, 0.94);
}
CONNECTOMICS & DENDRITIC COMPUTATION

Shapson-Coe et al. (2024, Science) · Gidon et al. (2020, Science)

Shapson-Coe and colleagues reconstructed ~1 mm³ of human temporal cortex at nanoscale resolution, revealing ~57,000 cells and ~150 million synapses. Separately, direct recordings from human layer 2/3 pyramidal dendrites demonstrated calcium-mediated dendritic action potentials with nonlinear input–output behavior. These findings motivate the atlas’ emphasis on dendrites as active computational compartments without assigning a single universal spine-neck resistance.

HUMAN NEURONAL α3 Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase // STRUCTURAL STATES

Human α3β1 Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase — PDB: 8D3V / 8D3U / 8D3Y / 8D3X

Cryo-EM structures of human neuronal α3 Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase capture inward-facing, Na⁺-occluded, exoplasmic-open, and K⁺-occluded conformations. In human α3 numbering, the catalytic phosphorylation site is Asp366. The atlas uses these states as a structural guide for an intentionally accelerated Post–Albers teaching cycle; it does not claim to replay atomic trajectories from a single PDB structure.

VOLTAGE-GATED NaV & KV STRUCTURAL REFERENCES

Human NaV1.4 — PDB: 6AGF · Mammalian KV1.2 — PDB: 8VC6

The NaV teaching model is anchored to the human NaV1.4 structure 6AGF and emphasizes voltage-sensor activation plus fast-inactivation coupling. The IFM motif is shown docking off-axis at its intracellular receptor pocket rather than physically plugging the central pore. The KV model is anchored to mammalian KV1.2 structure 8VC6; its selectivity-filter lesson uses the canonical TVGYG motif while keeping the 3D model explicitly illustrative rather than atom-for-atom.

CLINICAL PATHOLOGY // PREVIEW FOR A LATER RELEASE

Neurodegeneration, demyelination & ischemia

The disease modes in this cellular edition are qualitative previews only. Alzheimer disease is represented by amyloid plaques, tau pathology and dendritic/synaptic injury; multiple sclerosis by CNS demyelination with nodal/paranodal disruption; and ischemia by ATP failure, ionic-gradient collapse, depolarization and cytotoxic swelling. Disease-specific staging, molecular pathways and brain-territory anatomy are intentionally deferred to the dedicated clinical version.
